##Purpose

Our team aims to develop a well-researched and organized thesis proposal that meets academic requirements and is completed on time. We will work collaboratively, communicate effectively, and support one another throughout the research process to achieve our shared goals.

##Roles

Each member will be assigned specific responsibilities based on strengths and availability. The team leader will coordinate tasks, monitor progress, and ensure deadlines are met. Researchers will gather and evaluate sources, while writers and editors will prepare, revise, and format the proposal. All members are expected to contribute equally and assist others when necessary.

##Working Norms

The team will hold regular meetings at least once a week and additional meetings when needed. Members are expected to respond to messages within 24 hours and communicate any concerns or delays promptly. All documents and research materials will be stored in Github for easy access and collaboration.

##Decision-Making

The team will make decisions through open discussion. If an agreement cannot be reached, a majority vote will determine the final decision. All members will have an equal opportunity to share their opinions and suggestions.

##Conflict Process

Any conflicts or misunderstandings should first be addressed respectfully between the involved members. If the issue remains unresolved, it will be discussed with the entire team. If necessary, the instructor or adviser will be consulted for guidance and support.

##Quality and Tracking

Tasks will be assigned and tracked using a shared task list or progress tracker. Completed work will be reviewed by at least one other team member to ensure accuracy, quality, and consistency. All members are responsible for meeting deadlines and maintaining high standards throughout the project.
